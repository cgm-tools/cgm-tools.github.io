Quick-Look Tools for Circumgalactic Absorption-Line Spectroscopy

cgm-tools.github.io

Abstract

These pages provide curves of growth, ionization energies and resonance-line atomic data for the ions most often used to study the circumgalactic medium (CGM), from H I and the low ions through the EUV ladder (O II–O V, Ne V–Ne VIII, Mg X) to the X-ray lines of O VII and O VIII. Every wavelength, oscillator strength, damping constant and ionization energy is read by a script from a copy of the original compilation kept in this repository, and every value on the site links back to the row it came from. The line list holds — transitions of — ions of — elements.

Tools

Curve of growth

Rest-frame equivalent width against column density for up to six transitions at once, for any Doppler parameter, with reference curves at b = 10, 30 and 100 km s−1, a hover read-out, and a table of τ0, the damping parameter and Wr at a chosen column density. The Voigt profile is integrated over its full width, including the damping wings.

Ions and transitions

An ionization-energy ladder and a table of ionization energies, CIE peak temperatures and resonance lines for 20 elements, filterable by element and by UV, EUV, X-ray, optical and ISM/DLA lines.

Figures can be saved as PNG or vector PDF in a matplotlib-like style for slides and papers. Selections on the curve-of-growth page are stored in the URL, so a link reproduces the plot.

Data sources

The raw tables are kept unchanged in sources/. scripts/build_data.py reads them and writes data/lines.js and data/ions.js, which are all the pages use.

  • Line wavelengths, f-values and Γ (λ > 912 Å). Morton, D. C. 2003, ApJS, 149, 205 — Table 2, the AAS machine-readable file. ADS
  • EUV and X-ray lines, and Ca II. Verner, D. A., Verner, E. M., & Ferland, G. J. 1996, Atomic Data and Nuclear Data Tables, 64, 1 — Table 1. ADS (Calcium is missing from the published Morton 2003 machine-readable table.)
  • Alternative f-values (annotations only). Cashman, F. H., Kulkarni, V. P., Kisielius, R., Ferland, G. J., & Bogdanovich, P. 2017, ApJS, 230, 8. ADS
  • Ionization energies. Kramida, A., Ralchenko, Yu., Reader, J., & NIST ASD Team, NIST Atomic Spectra Database, physics.nist.gov/asd (retrieved 2026 November 11).
  • CIE ion fractions. Gnat, O., & Sternberg, A. 2007, ApJS, 168, 213 (via VizieR J/ApJS/168/213). ADS
  • Voigt function. Humlíček, J. 1982, JQSRT, 27, 437. ADS

Where Morton (2003) and Verner et al. (1996) both list a line, the build cross-checks the two, and it also flags lines whose Cashman et al. (2017) f-value differs by more than 10%. The results are in crosscheck_report.txt. DATA_CHANGES.md lists every value that differs from the first version of this site.

Rebuilding the data

python3 scripts/build_data.py         # sources/ → data/
python3 scripts/compare_legacy.py     # DATA_CHANGES.md
python  scripts/make_reference_cog.py # test reference (scipy)

To add a transition, add a row to scripts/line_selection.txt (element, ion, approximate wavelength, group) and rerun the build. The script looks the line up in the source tables and stops if no entry lies within 0.05 Å.
